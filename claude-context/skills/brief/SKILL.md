# Brief — 60-Second Status

Deliver in this order, no padding:

1. **Top priority right now** — one sentence, based on open projects and today's daily note.
2. **Next 3 things on the plate** — pulled from Projects/ and Daily Notes/.
3. **Open loops** — commitments made that haven't been closed.
4. **Anything blocked** — flag it and suggest an unblock.
5. **Business pulse** — one line on Big Bigger Media: any client deadlines, pitches, or follow-ups due?

Total response: under 150 words. Direct. No bullet padding.
