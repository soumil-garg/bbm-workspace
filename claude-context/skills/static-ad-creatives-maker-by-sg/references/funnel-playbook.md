# Funnel playbook: top vs bottom

## Top of funnel (cold)
First decide category familiarity (SKILL.md Stage 0). If the category is known (toothpaste, deo), cold viewers are problem- to solution-aware: sceptical, burned by claims, so lead with a recognisable moment or honest tension, not the deal. If the category is NEW or known under another name (e.g. body glue), most cold viewers are unaware of the product type itself, so top of funnel has two layers:
- **Explain set (at least half):** the image itself must state the category name, what it does and the problem, in plain words. Formats: demo shot, "What is X?" explainer, "No A. No B. Just X.", problem/fix split, notes-style explainer, cardboard-sign explainer. Keep text minimal: one definition line plus the headline.
- **Hook set:** the recognition, enemy, mechanism and curiosity concepts below. These assume the viewer knows the product type, so treat them as problem-aware / middle-of-funnel for new categories.
The Habbits concepts below are hooks from a known category.
- Concepts (Habbits round 1): "One tube won't do it", "No, it won't work in 1 day", "Zoomed into the group photo", "Stop putting lemon on your teeth", "The black powder everyone doubts", WhatsApp family.
- Formats: native (notes, chat), real people with gaze, bold type, ugly slot, product-hero contrast.
- Offer/deal appears as a small pill, not the headline (if client mandates a deal).
- Test order (from the testing-engine plan): offer (core promise) first, then angle, then format, then execution.

## Bottom of funnel (warm, offer-only)
Audience knows the brand. Goal: highlight the offer as clearly as possible.
- The offer is the headline: "PICK ANY 3. PAY FOR 2."
- Show it is ANY, not a fixed pack: show the full range (8 products), a pick action, or a cart.
- Formats that worked: minimal range shot (M), bold type on black (T), native cart with the free line at Rs 0 (N), price-card combos (P, keep it to 2-3 cards), hand picking from a shelf (F, for women-targeted), kirana cardboard sign in Hinglish (U).
- Combos: pre-built bundles cut choice overload; show only the pay-price, no struck prices when overfilled.
- Numbers: rupees over percent; anchor on the full price; compute from live prices.
- Missing lever to ask about: end date / deadline. Do not invent scarcity.
- Do not use problem-agitation or heavy proof; they belong upstream.

## Sizes and placements
1:1 (1254x1254 from ChatGPT) for Feed. For Stories/Reels make 9:16 only for approved winners, as ONE ad with placement asset customization (see Stage 10).

## Sets to produce by default
Both funnels: 6 TOF concepts x 2-3 systems, 6 BOF creatives. Recommend 3 BOF (cart, combos, native sign) and one per TOF concept.
