# Daigo round 2: independent critic read (Hinglish headlines)

Scoring key (1 to 5): a = a real 22 year old would say or share it, b = Hinglish reads natural, c = stops the scroll at 4 words or fewer on a phone (longer lines lose points here), d = one idea only. FAIL = breaks the banned list or the facts list, implied claims included. "OK (watch)" = passes, but a strict claims reader could squint at it.

## WEAKEST TWO CONCEPTS TO CUT
1. C8 THIRTY PACK: every candidate is a count or a price ("30 sachet. 30 glass. Rs 749."), the exact flat style he rejected, and its best line is only fine. Rs 749 and Rs 25 a glass can ride as a strip on a stronger scene.

## WHAT THE SET IS MISSING
1. No office or road-trip concept: "office" is in the verified facts and "ROAD TRIP? CHAAS IS PACKED." was a September favourite, yet every scene is hostel, PG or one train (keep out slump, energy, focus, heat).
2. No loud over-the-top line in the style he approved ("SOOOOOOOO MUCH WATER!!!"): everything here is deadpan, so write one stretched-letter shout over the glass and test it.
3. No ritual or sound concept that avoids taste altogether (the tear, the shake, the steel glass clink): the method is only explained, never enjoyed.
4. The same two facts (no dahi, no mixer) carry C1, C3, C10 and C11, and four of twelve concepts are pack or price spec sheets (C5, C6, C8, C9), so the set repeats itself.
5. Many candidates break the 2 to 7 word headline brief (C1 lines 6 and 7, C3 line 6, C9 lines 1 and 7, C10 line 3, all of C11), and "Apni chaas" appears in two concepts: state the cap in the next writer brief and use the template once.
