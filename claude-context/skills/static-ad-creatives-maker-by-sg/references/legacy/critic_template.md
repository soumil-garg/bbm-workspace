## The brand and the buyer

Buyers: 20 to 30 year olds in Indian metros who are away from home (hostel, PG, first job, trains, road trips, office). They read and speak Hinglish and English and switch freely. The ads are static Meta ads, 4:5, with ONE big headline (2 to 7 words, 2 to 5 is best) and sometimes one small supporting line. The PICTURE IS ALREADY DESIGNED AND APPROVED (scene given per concept); the line must make that picture land, so headline, picture and product are one story.
