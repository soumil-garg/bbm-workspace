## BLURB
Daigo sells an instant chaas (spiced buttermilk) mix in single-serve sachets (Masala flavour). You tear a sachet, add cold water, shake, and you have a glass. Buyers: 20 to 30 year olds in Indian metros who are away from home (hostel, PG, first job, trains, road trips, office). They read and speak Hinglish and English and switch freely. The ads are static Meta ads, 4:5, with ONE big headline (2 to 7 words, 2 to 5 is best) and sometimes one small supporting line. The PICTURE IS ALREADY DESIGNED AND APPROVED (scene given per concept); the line must make that picture land, so headline, picture and product are one story.

## WHY
- Round 1 copy (literal, claims-safe) was rejected. Round 2 copy was "a little better". The founder says the graphic designs are now "really, really better" but wants BETTER copywriting, and a MIX of Hinglish and English, not Hinglish only.
- He ruled out "Phaado. Daalo. Hilao." (Hinglish for tear, pour, shake): "very controversial". The three verbs in that order can be read as a crude innuendo. It got through an earlier critic because nobody screened for double meanings. Daigo lines he liked in September (voice, not claims): "TRAIN KA KHANA. GHAR KI CHAAS.", "ROAD TRIP? CHAAS IS PACKED.", "EK PACKET OPEN KRO. EK GLASS READY." (the old ones used claims that are now banned).
- The previous critic found the set repetitive (the same two facts carried four concepts) and too deadpan; a template such as "X ka khana. Apni chaas." may be used once at most.

## FACTS
- Pack directions: tear, add cold water, shake. A pocket-size sachet. Chaas mix. Masala flavour.
- One sachet makes one glass. 10 sachets = 10 glasses; 20 = 20; 30 = 30. Prices Rs 279 (10), Rs 529 (20), Rs 749 (30). Per glass Rs 28, Rs 26, Rs 25. So "10 glasses for under Rs 300" and "under Rs 30 a glass" are true.
- No curd to set, no blender or mixer to wash, no mess.
- Occasions: train, trip, PG, hostel, office. The feeling "when you miss ghar ki chaas" is allowed as a feeling, never as a taste claim or a replacement.
- Extra 5% off prepaid with code PREPAY5 (the 20-pack ad only). No delivery or free-shipping wording anywhere.

## BANNED
real, fresh, natural, authentic, pure, genuine, "ghar jaisa" / "just like home" / "same taste as homemade" (any taste equivalence), probiotic, gut, digestion, acidity, bloating, calcium, protein, healthy, health, superfood, guilt-free, low calorie, energy, slump, focus, hydrate, electrolytes, cooling, heat relief, first / pioneer / only, any seconds or minutes ("10 seconds", "1 minute", "in no time"), "zero preservatives", "no preservatives", "no fridge needed", comparisons with other products or brands (tetra pack, packaged buttermilk, coffee, chai), vrat / fasting / Jain / Navratri, "limited time", countdowns, any customer quote or review, any health or weight claim, any claim about taste quality ("tastes great", "best chaas"). Also avoid insulting the reader, and anything about alcohol, religion, caste, region stereotypes, politics or breaking hostel rules.
