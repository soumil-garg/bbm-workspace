| N4 Another meeting. Another sachet. | FLAG | LOW | "Another X. Another [habit]" is the stock coping-drink joke ("another day, another drink"). In India sachets are also what pan masala and gutka come in, so "another sachet" can read as a chewing habit through the workday. | "Another meeting. Another chaas break." |
| N5 Ek chaas toh banti hai. | FLAG | LOW | Yes, it echoes the alcohol idiom. The frame "ek ___ toh banta hai" is the same one used for "ek peg toh banta hai" (and "ek sutta toh banta hai"). The chai version is the everyday one, so most readers hear "treat yourself" and the echo is faint. Keep it away from night, clink, bar or party cues, and never follow it with "aur ek?". | If kept: daytime desk or lunch scene only. Safer: "Chaas ka time ho gaya." |
