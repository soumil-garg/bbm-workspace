# Design systems: one idea, several valid looks

Render the SAME copy in 2-3 of these per concept so the ad test isolates design. Each has a reason it works. Prompt blocks live in `scripts/prompts_template.py` (SYS dict).

| Code | System | Why it works | What it looks like | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M | Minimal Swiss | Fewer competing elements hold attention (Pieters, Wedel & Batra 2010) | Off-white, products, huge headline, one accent element | premium brands, BOF |
| T | Bold typography | Easy-to-read text is trusted and liked (Reber & Schwarz, fluency) | Headline fills about 60% of canvas, flush left, small products | short punchy claims, strongest scroll stop |
| N | Native lo-fi | People ignore anything that looks like an ad (banner blindness) | Notes app page, WhatsApp chat, cart screenshot, phone flash photo | sceptical audiences, TOF |
| F | Face and gaze | Faces grab attention first and viewers follow the gaze (Cerf et al. 2008) | Person looking at the product/offer, headline in empty space | emotion, women-targeted sets |
| H | Product hero contrast | One isolated item pops out (Von Restorff) | One product or group lit alone, others desaturated or crossed out | enemy/comparison angles |
| P | Proof and data | Concrete numbers feel credible | Receipt, price cards, progress bar; numbers are the hero | BOF, value angles |
| A | Rich art direction | Design complexity in ONE idea can help attention (same 2010 study) | Styled real scene, editorial light, still one headline | brand feel, TOF |
| U | Deliberately ugly | Pattern interrupt: nothing looks like an ad | MS Paint, marker on cardboard, sticky notes, flat flash | scroll-stopping slot in every set |

## Ugly ads rules
Ugly in design, never in clarity. The offer still reads in 2 seconds. Brand products must still be faithful. Keep humour non-gross (Meta health-content policy: no extreme teeth/body close-ups, no before/after that implies guaranteed results, no pointing at viewer attributes).

## Picking systems per concept
- Emotional/moment angle: F, A, N.
- Enemy/myth-bust: H, T, U.
- Value/offer: P, M, N (cart), U (kirana sign).
- Skeptic/proof: T, F, H.
Always include one U or N per set.
