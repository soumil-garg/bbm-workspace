# Weekly Review

1. Aggregate the last 7 daily notes. Summarize: what got done, what slipped, what was avoided.
2. For each active Project in `Projects/`, give a 1-line status: on track / slipping / blocked.
3. Check `Goals/` — is the 90-day goal (streamline ops with AI → close more clients) on track?
4. Highlight 3 wins, 3 things avoided.
5. Propose next week's ONE big thing with a reason.
6. **Vocab note:** Surface 2–3 things from this week's conversations where Soumil's phrasing could have been more precise or impactful. Be specific — quote the phrase, suggest the improvement.
7. **Mood check:** If any pattern of low energy or distraction appeared in the daily notes this week, flag it and suggest one concrete thing to address it.
8. Save the review as `Goals/Weekly-<YYYY-WW>.md`.

Be direct. Pick a winner for next week and defend it.
