On ad creative work, a strategy brief alone is an incomplete answer. Soumil produces creative with **AI UGC tooling, not shoots** — always cover how the creative actually gets made, and check for connectable MCPs/skills before concluding.

- **Arcads** — MCP at `https://mcp.arcads.ai`. NOT in the Claude connector registry; must be added as a custom connector (Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector). Skills plugin: `/spy-competitor-ads`, `/clone-hook`, `/clone-static-ad`. Best talking-head realism (mocap avatars). ~$110/mo per 10 videos.
- **Higgsfield** — `npx skills add higgsfield-ai/skills` then `higgsfield auth login`. 30+ models, Marketing Studio, AI Ad Generator from product URL, Popcorn storyboarding. $15/$39/$99 per month. Far better volume-per-rupee.

**Why:** On 2026-07-19 I delivered a Daigo creative brief that was strategically sound off live Meta data but never mentioned either tool, and cited H2 2025 benchmarks as current. He called it surface-level, and was right — the brief assumed a shoot he does not do, and I never checked whether the production tools were connectable.

**How to apply:** For any creative deliverable — (1) check the MCP registry AND search the web for a direct MCP/skills integration before saying a capability is unavailable; the registry is incomplete and misses custom connectors. (2) Date-check every benchmark; say the vintage out loud. (3) End creative briefs with a production routing table (which tool for which asset) and a cost line, not just angles and hooks. Ties to his 90-day goal: removing the shoot from the critical path is the bandwidth win. See [[process_ad_creatives]], [[client_daigo]], [[feedback_blockers]].
