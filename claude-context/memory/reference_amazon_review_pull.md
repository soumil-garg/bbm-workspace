**Method (skill playbook section 2):** Claude in Chrome on the profile logged into Amazon, in-page fetch loops over /product-reviews/ASIN with filterByStar and filterByKeyword, results POSTed to a localhost receiver (C:\cca\habbits\step2\recv2.py on 127.0.0.1:8765, detached PID 42172 on 2026-10-07; stop it when done). Only ~10 reviews per query, so depth comes from star x sort x keyword combos. Yield seen 2026-10-07: Perfora Purple 125g gave 124 unique reviews (95 critical) from 69 queries in about a minute; Habbits' own listings are tiny (6 to 30 reviews each). Speed was healthy (about 1.3 fetches per second, no throttle in 251 fetches).

**RISK (verified 2026-10-07, ShopiFreaks report dated 3 Aug 2026):** Amazon silently limits accounts its anti-scraping flags as bots to 8 reviews per product; no notice; fix is an email appeal and several business days; Amazon calls it rare and will not say what triggers it. The bulk pull uses Soumil's own account, so I PAUSED the pull at 7 listings (401 reviews before dedupe, 292 critical, 251 fetches) and told him. Do not run big pulls on his personal account without telling him this first. Earlier heavy pulls (Habbits 29 Sep, Savvy 5 Oct, Lotus 6 Oct) already used it.

**Paid options (what I could verify, 2026-10-07):** DataForSEO Merchant Amazon Reviews API: India supported, star filters 1-5 plus positive and critical, keyword search, reviewer type, max 50 reviews per task, billed per 10 reviews, sort helpful only, async; price reported $0.00075 per 10 reviews on the standard queue (about $0.075 per 1,000 reviews, up to 45 minutes turnaround), minimum deposit not found. Bright Data Amazon Reviews scraper: pay per successful record from $0.75 per 1,000, takes product URLs, India, star filter and limits not stated in the docs I could read. Oxylabs: Amazon scraping about $0.50 per 1,000 results, free trial reported 2,000 results, reviews specifics not verified. Unwrangle: 1 credit per request, vendor says about 500 reviews per product by combining filters, $99 plan 100k requests, amazon.in not verified. Apify review actors $0.50 to $5 per 1,000 reviews, but our own 6 Oct test of one returned about 20 per star per listing with 38% duplicates. Rainforest API has a reviews endpoint, limits not read. All vendors face the same Amazon limits (login wall, about 100 visible reviews per filter combination), so they only do the combining, login and proxies for you; test one big listing before committing. Soumil has said money is no object for this; signing up and entering payment are his steps, never mine.
