On 12 Oct 2026 he sent himself a Sereko Story ad (pigmentation lip balm) and said why he liked it: "the overall concept ... the image is very hooky as they speak to smokers and the tube is held as a cigarette." Filed in the library as Sereko_manual_20261012_1 with the chips Idea and Photo and his words in the taste profile.

**Why it matters:** the picture itself names the buyer before any headline is read. The product takes a gesture that belongs to the target (a tube held like a cigarette, lips in profile) so the right person stops and the wrong person scrolls. It is a visual metaphor, not a literal product shot, and the headline then adds a specific claim and proof.

**How to apply:** when writing concept sheets for any brand, ask "what gesture, prop or posture does my buyer already own?" and look for a version where the product stands in for it, so headline, picture and product stay one story ([[feedback-whole-ad-generation-harmony]]). Keep the claims inside the brand's claim register: the Sereko headline ("reverse smoker lips in 14 days") is a time-bound results promise that our copy check would block for a client.
