Soumil (2026-10-06): no post-purchase survey for clients, even a 3-question one, because nothing should interrupt the client's post-purchase experience. Approved Stage 0 asks: client review-app exports, Google Business Profile access, the brand's own ad comments, target CPA per account.

**Why:** protecting the client's customer experience and conversion/retention flow matters more than extra first-party data.
**How to apply:** do not install or propose Zigpoll/Fairing/KnoCommerce/Formbricks-style post-purchase surveys; for ICP verification use orders, reviews, comments, Meta breakdowns and customer interviews instead. A MaxDiff survey of about 50 non-customers or opted-in people is a different thing and stays optional, only on Soumil's say-so. See [[process_ad_creatives]], [[client_habbits]].
