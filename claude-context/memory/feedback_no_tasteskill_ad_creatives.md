Soumil rejected the "taste skill" (tasteskill.dev, Leonxlnx/taste-skill, a website-UI design framework) after I applied its rules to Lotus Sculpture ad creatives: "you fucked up the entire font thing". Nothing was installed; I only read its rules. Do not apply it, cite it, or re-suggest it for ads.

What he DID like (round C): the ChatGPT Plus scene ads where the statue is the hero in a dark studio with one spotlight and a modest sans headline top-left ("One of one." on the Thai Buddha, "Hand-cast, not machine-cast." on the bronze Ganesh). He disliked the PIL-built photo-plus-text cards with Segoe UI Light type.

**Why:** the PIL typography looked weak and the layouts felt generic; ChatGPT scene ads looked far more premium.
**How to apply:** for premium/handcrafted brands generate scene ads with ChatGPT Plus (check the profile first, see [[feedback_chatgpt_plus_profile]]) using the real product photo as reference; avoid local PIL typography for final creatives unless he asks. Related: [[process_ad_creatives]].
