On 9 Oct 2026 Soumil rejected the whole Daigo round 1: "i genuinely hate the ones u just made, the formats maybe good, but the text is not going with the overall vibe and then these are just really shit text and copyrighting ones", and said the ones made before the skill upgrade (September model-lettered AI statics) were better.

**Why (my diagnosis, not yet confirmed by him):** (1) copy was written from the claims-safe list, so every line was literal and compliant but had no voice ("10 sachets. 10 glasses of chaas."); the Hinglish, spoken voice of the category was lost; (2) all words were flat code typography dropped on a generated scene, which reads as a template, while the September batch had condensed heavy lettering drawn into the scene; (3) no people in the scenes, while his taste is real people and native formats; (4) I ran the critic for compliance and clarity, not for "would a real person say this".

**How to apply:** write the voice FIRST (Hinglish where the audience speaks it, one best line plus one alternative per concept, read aloud), then run the claim register and policy check as a FILTER that fixes wording, never as the writer; show the lines to him as plain text before any image is made; put real people in scenes (superseded 10 Oct: his approved ads do not favour people, see [[feedback-whole-ad-generation-harmony]]); test model-drawn, scene-integrated lettering (condensed heavy display type) against code text on the same copy; read his Swipe Desk taste profile ([[project-swipe-desk]]) before writing; never hold the creative quality for a technicality. Keep the claim bans (real, fresh, natural, health, probiotic, gut, digestion, calcium, protein, first, 10 seconds, ghar jaisa).

**Follow-up 10 Oct:** round 2 followed these rules ([[project-daigo-pilot-oct2026]]): lines written first and scored by an independent critic, real people in 11 of 16, lettering drawn into the picture on 6 posters, loud saturated look with a big pack. He rejected it that evening too (pasted look, no harmony, plain sachets): see [[feedback-whole-ad-generation-harmony]]; the real-people idea was a guess his approved ads do not support. Reusable lesson: look at what he approves in the Swipe Desk before designing, and keep an offer or hostel/train scene concept only if its line passes the critic.
