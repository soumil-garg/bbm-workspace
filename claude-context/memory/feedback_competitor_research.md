Never use keyword-anchored research alone when mapping competitors. Brands don't always call their product what the client calls theirs (e.g., Food Darzee calls tiffins "healthy meal subscription" — missed entirely by searching "tiffin").

**Full competitor discovery checklist (run every time, no shortcuts):**

1. **Generic listicles** — LBB, Magicpin, Lytmeals, JustDial, Sulekha for the city + category. Gets 60–70% of known brands. NOT sufficient alone.

2. **Customer discovery search** — Ask: "Where does a customer in this market actually go when they want to find this product?" Search there. The answer changes every time — it could be Amazon, a marketplace, an aggregator, Google Maps, a review site, or nothing (D2C-only brands won't be on any platform). Don't assume a platform. Figure it out per client.

3. **Google Maps search** — "tiffin service near [client's delivery area]" pulls hyper-local businesses with no online presence.

4. **Instagram hashtag discovery** — Search #[city][category] variations: #mumbaitiffin, #tiffinservicemumbai, #mumbaifood, #lowerparel, #worlifood. True Tiffins was only discoverable this way — no website, no listicle, found via hashtag.

5. **Ads Library keyword sweep** — Run 10+ keyword variations including:
   - Category name (tiffin, dabba)
   - Adjacent categories (meal subscription, diet meals, healthy meals, lunch delivery, food delivery)
   - Delivery mechanic (dabbawala, home-cooked, chef-supervised)
   - Geo keywords (mumbai, city name + meal)
   Missed Food Darzee because only "tiffin"-anchored terms were searched initially.

6. **Competitor cross-reference** — Look at who each found competitor follows on Instagram, who follows them, whose posts they like. Competitors know each other.

7. **Search with brand-agnostic terms on Google** — "tiffin service Lower Parel" not just "tiffin service Mumbai" — hyper-local Google searches surface brands invisible to city-level searches.

**Why:** After full research for Moiras, missed True Tiffins (no website, Instagram-only) and Food Darzee (227K IG, located in Lower Parel, markets as "healthy meal subscription" not "tiffin") because research was listicle + narrow keyword anchored. User corrected directly.

**How to apply:** For every new client, run ALL 7 steps before declaring the competitor map complete. Do not present partial lists as complete lists. If a step is blocked (e.g., Zomato requires a browser), flag it and use workaround.
