Soumil (2026-10-04): "from now on dont differentiate between my private and public repo coz no one's coming to my repo so dont worry."

**Why:** he judges the exposure risk as nil and the sanitising and split-repo work as wasted effort.

**How to apply:** do not sanitise client names, swap in "Brand A/B", strip third-party ad images, or keep separate public and private copies of the skill and library. Put everything (skill, inspiration library with images, client context) in one place and sync it as-is. This overrides the older rule in [[reference_github_repos]] that client work must never go in the public repo. Still never write real secrets (API keys, tokens) into any repo.
