Since Aug 2026 Soumil is producing data-driven Instagram reels for his own page (separate from Big Bigger Media client work). Topics so far: highest-paying jobs (India vs US), India property ROI by area since 1980.

The workflow that worked and he did not push back on:
1. Multi-source web research with real citations.
2. Save the full research to `Vault/Resources/<topic>.md` with a sources list, reel angles, and an on-screen caveats section.
3. Build a **9:16 (1080×1920) animated artifact** he screen-records — scene sequence with a countdown, a graph payoff frame, a twist frame, and a caveats/CTA end card. Controls sit outside the recorded frame.

Standing preferences observed:
- He wants the counterintuitive angle, not the obvious ranking.
- He wants caveats on screen — they pre-empt the comment section.
- Put 2–3 source links in the caption to kill "made-up numbers" comments.

**Why:** he is building audience on his own page, so credibility and a repeatable production pipeline matter more than speed.
**How to apply:** when he says "next reel idea", default to this three-step pipeline. Ask about framing only when the baseline assumption (start year, market, metric) would materially change the output.
