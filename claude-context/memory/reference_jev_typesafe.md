"Jev" in Soumil's speech = TypeSafe AI's Jev ("System One") model: hosted text classifier, typed yes/no, pick-one or score questions in, probabilities out, no prose, no images. $0.042 per M input tokens, output free; one live version jev-1.13.0 (pin it, not jev-latest).

**Verdict (deep research 2026-10-07):** a tryout, not a job. YES as a second reader for review-theme labelling and ad-comment classification, YES as a one-off Upworthy headline backtest, MAYBE as a one-way copy-compliance flagger, NO for awareness levels and competitor ad-text tagging, Hinglish unverified (treat as NO). Jev gives no reasons or quotes, so a small chat model stays the primary labeller. Full report: vault Resources/Jev for the Ad Engine - Deep Research.md.
