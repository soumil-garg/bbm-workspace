Soumil (2026-10-01, Savvy Body Glue), rejecting picks 9, 12, 14, 19 and the tool call to upload them: "let's say I don't know that a product like body glue exists... 'Tape came off in 10 minutes' I don't know if that's body tape or how it relates to a cloth sticking to your skin... 'Dance, don't adjust' does not signal that your blouse won't fall off... good for someone who knows what Savvy is, not for someone who does not know the concept of body glue exists." He wants TOF creatives that explain in the crux what the product is, the problem and what it does.

**Why:** cold viewers have no category knowledge. Emotional or clever hooks (dance, tape, maang tika, safety pins alone) assume they know the product. Even round 1b "unaware" lines like "There's a glue for that" were too implicit.

**How to apply:** every TOF creative must state in plain words, in the image, (1) the category ("body glue"), (2) what it does ("sticks your clothes to your skin"), (3) the problem (strap slipping, neckline gaping). Test: show it to someone who has never heard of the product; can they say what it is in 2 seconds? Use demo shots, plain-language headlines, explainer formats. Keep clever or emotional hooks for MOF/BOF only. See [[process_ad_creatives]].
