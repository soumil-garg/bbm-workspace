# Static ad design rules (v1, 29 Sep 2026)

Built after Soumil's feedback on the Habbits Buy 2 Get 1 set: "overfilled", "hard to focus", "headline should get the most focus", copy "hard to understand".

## Sources
- Pieters, Wedel & Batra (2010), "The Stopping Power of Advertising", Journal of Marketing. Eye-tracking on 249 ads: feature complexity (dense objects, text, colours) HURTS attention to the brand and ad liking; design complexity (one elaborate creative idea) HELPS attention and comprehension.
- Owl-Listener/designer-skills, visual-critique: one primary emphasis per view; size jump of at least 1.5x between hierarchy levels; no elements with equal weight but unequal importance; cut anything that does not serve the one goal.
- Meta creative best practices 2026: one clear hero element; overlay only one headline or offer; heavy text overlays lose reach.
- Anthropic canvas-design skill: breathing room is non-negotiable; nothing overlaps; limited, intentional palette.
- 60-30-10 colour rule; CRAP (Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, Proximity).

## Hard rules for every creative
1. ONE idea, ONE focal point. The headline is the entry point.
2. Max 3 text elements: headline, optional subline, optional offer tag. Max ~12 words on the whole canvas.
3. Headline: 2-6 words, readable and understood in 1 second. No wordplay that needs decoding.
4. Headline at least 3x the size of any other text. Subline and tag are clearly secondary.
5. At least 40% of the canvas is empty negative space.
6. Max 3 products, one tidy group. No decorative props (leaves, flowers, charcoal, stones, bangles) unless the idea needs them.
7. 60-30-10 colour: background 60%, products 30%, accent 10%. The accent is used on ONE element only (the offer).
8. Single alignment axis (all centred or all left). Z-path: headline, then products, then offer.
9. Even, generous spacing between headline, products and offer.
10. Real people: max 2 people, exactly 2 arms and 2 hands each, product large and legible.
11. Squint test: blurred, only the headline and the products should still read.
