# Psychology framework (Stage 3)

## Vocabulary (locked)
Worked example: onion hair oil for men.

| Term | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Segment | Who? | Men 24-32, hairline starting to recede |
| Driver | What do they feel? | Fear of looking older, embarrassed in photos |
| Offer | What does the creative promise? (core promise, NOT a discount) | "Less hair fall in 30 days" |
| Angle | Through what lens do we pitch it? | "The group photo moment" |
| Format | What does it look like? | Notes-app screenshot, UGC selfie |
| Hook | What stops the scroll? | "Zoomed into my wedding photos. Then I saw my crown." |
| Execution | How is it built? | Colours, layout, copy length |

Concept = Segment x Driver x Offer x Angle. Format, hook, execution dress the concept.
Offer = destination. Angle = door in. Same offer through many angles; same angle can carry different offers.

Offer types: result, speed, ease, safety, cheaper than alternative, certainty/proof, identity/status.
Angle types: moment of realisation, enemy (blame something), cost of inaction, comparison vs alternative, myth-busting, social proof, identity, curiosity.

## When the client fixes a deal (e.g. Buy 2 Get 1 Free)
The deal is constant. The variable becomes the reason to want the deal. Habbits offers: complete routine, enough to see results (results take weeks), ready for the moment, clean/safe, fraction of the professional price, try it (3rd on us), share it.

## Awareness (Schwartz) decides the headline
| Level | Lead with | Funnel |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | story, curiosity, identity | TOF |
| Problem-aware | the pain in their words | TOF |
| Solution-aware | why this beats what they tried | TOF/MOF |
| Product-aware | proof, comparison, objection removal | MOF |
| Most aware | the offer itself | BOF |
Sophistication: if the category has heard every claim ("instant", "whitens in 1 use"), do not escalate the claim. Use honesty, mechanism, or identity instead.

## Segment table (per segment)
Segment | driver | main objection | awareness | evidence (HIGH/MED/LOW + source). Build 4-5 segments from Stage 1 + 2 data. Note delivery drift (which segments the current creatives fail to reach) and build a share of creatives for under-served, high-converting segments.

## Offer bank
Rank 5-7 offers by evidence, each with promise, segments, and evidence source. Prefer offers with a verifiable basis (brand's own directions, past winners, reviews).

## Angles
3-4 per offer, each a one-line headline idea plus the format that suits it. Mark which need data we do not have.

## Guardrails (always list)
- Framing traps for the category (e.g. whitening in India reads as a fairness cream: never "whiter = more beautiful"; say stains off / your clean smile back).
- Platform policy (Meta personal attributes: do not point at the viewer's body, health, finances).
- Claims competitors own (do not lead with them).
- Items to verify before use: claims, prices, cart rules, end dates.

## Recommended round
One offer per creative, told plainly, plus one native/ugly slot. Show it, recommend, wait for GATE 1.
